
Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance, customer behavior, regional sales trends, and product profitability.

The goal is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify high-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Understand customer segment contributions.
- Compare regional sales and profit performance.
- Investigate loss-making products and discount patterns.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to support business decisions.

Tools & Technologies

-Python: Data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis.
- Pandas: Data manipulation and analysis.
- SQL / MySQL: Data querying and business analysis.
- Power BI:Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
- Jupyter Notebook: Python analysis environment.

Project Workflow

1. Imported the Sample Superstore dataset.
2. Cleaned and transformed the data using Python and Pandas.
3. Performed SQL-based business analysis.
4. Conducted exploratory data analysis.
5. Created an interactive Power BI dashboard.
6. Identified business insights and recommendations.

Key Business Findings

- Total sales were approximately $2.30 million.
- Total profit was approximately $286.40 thousand.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 12.47%.
- Technology was the highest-performing category by sales and profit.
- The West region generated the highest regional sales and profit.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies recorded negative profits.
- Higher discount bands were associated with negative aggregate profit in this dataset.

Business Recommendations

- Review discount policies and their impact on profitability.
- Investigate pricing and operating costs for loss-making sub-categories.
- Study successful regional strategies to improve performance elsewhere.
- Monitor profit margins alongside revenue growth.
- Evaluate customer segments to identify growth opportunities.
